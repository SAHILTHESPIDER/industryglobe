# 🌍 Industry Globe

**Build Your Career. Own the Studios. Launch Your Streaming Empire. Conquer the Global Entertainment Industry.**

Industry Globe is an entertainment industry life-simulation and business strategy game where players can build their journey from an aspiring actor to a global entertainment mogul. Manage your acting career, produce movies and series, establish production houses, launch streaming platforms, compete with rival companies, and expand your entertainment empire across the world.

Every decision shapes your career, reputation, finances, relationships, and the future of your entertainment empire.

## 🎬 About the Game

Industry Globe combines celebrity life simulation, entertainment production, business management, and global streaming competition in one evolving game world.

Start small, build your reputation, and grow into a powerful industry leader. Whether you want to become a superstar actor, a successful filmmaker, a production house owner, or the founder of a global streaming platform, the industry is yours to shape.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎭 1. Acting & Celebrity Life
- Start your journey as an aspiring actor.
- Attend auditions and secure acting opportunities.
- Work in movies, television shows, and web series.
- Build fame, reputation, popularity, and professional relationships.
- Participate in premieres, press tours, film festivals, and award ceremonies.
- Compete with rival actors and industry personalities.
- Develop your career through strategic decisions and performance success.

### 🎥 2. Production House Management
- Establish and manage your own production house.
- Develop and produce movies, television shows, and web series.
- Manage production budgets, casting, creative decisions, and project schedules.
- Invest in new projects and develop original intellectual properties.
- Build relationships with actors, directors, writers, and other industry professionals.
- Compete against rival production houses.
- Grow from a small independent studio into a major entertainment company.

### 📺 3. Streaming Platform Empire
- Build and manage your own streaming platform.
- Develop a library of movies, shows, and exclusive originals.
- Compete with rival streaming services for viewers and market share.
- Manage subscriptions, pricing strategies, content investments, and platform growth.
- Track subscribers, revenue, audience engagement, and content performance.
- Acquire or license entertainment content where supported.
- Expand your platform into new markets and compete in the global streaming industry.

### 🌎 4. Global Entertainment Expansion
- Expand your business into international entertainment markets.
- Establish production operations in different regions.
- Build a global distribution and streaming presence.
- Develop content for diverse audiences and markets.
- Compete with international studios and streaming companies.
- Grow your influence across the entertainment industry.

### 💰 5. Business, Finance & Investments
- Manage personal wealth and company finances.
- Invest in entertainment projects and business opportunities.
- Balance production costs, operating expenses, and revenue.
- Make strategic decisions to increase profitability.
- Expand your portfolio through studios, platforms, and other available assets.
- Build long-term financial stability and industry influence.

### 🏆 6. Fame, Awards & Industry Competition
- Earn recognition through successful projects.
- Compete for awards and professional achievements.
- Build your reputation among audiences and industry professionals.
- Track competitors and respond to changing industry conditions.
- Establish a lasting name in entertainment.

### 📱 7. In-Game Digital World
Explore an in-game digital ecosystem featuring systems such as:
- Social media and public reputation
- Messaging and professional communication
- Dating and relationships
- Stocks and financial activities
- Career opportunities and industry interactions
- Business and entertainment management

### 🏡 8. Lifestyle & Personal Assets
- Purchase homes, cars, and luxury assets.
- Upgrade your lifestyle as your wealth grows.
- Balance personal spending with business investments.
- Build a life beyond your professional career.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 9. Relationships, Family & Legacy
- Develop personal and professional relationships.
- Build a family and experience changing family dynamics.
- Continue your journey through the next generation where supported.
- Preserve wealth, influence, and family legacy over time.

## 🧠 Gameplay Philosophy

Industry Globe is designed around long-term progression and meaningful choices.

Your success depends on how you manage your career, finances, productions, businesses, relationships, and global expansion. Build your own strategy: become an entertainment superstar, create a powerful production company, dominate streaming, or combine all three into a worldwide empire.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js
- npm

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/SAHILTHESPIDER/industryglobe.git
   ```

2. Navigate to the application directory:

   ```bash
   cd industryglobe/Actor-Empire-main
   ```

3. Install dependencies:

   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

4. Start the development server:

   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

5. Open the local URL displayed in your terminal, usually:

   ```text
   http://localhost:5173
   ```

## 🏗️ Build & Quality Checks

Build the application:

```bash
npm run build
```

Run the project's configured lint checks:

```bash
npm run lint
```

## ⚙️ Environment Configuration

Optional local environment variables may include:

- `VITE_DEV_TOOLS_PASSCODE` — configures the developer tools passcode.
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` — available for locally configured Gemini-powered functionality.

Store private credentials in a local `.env.local` file. Do not commit API keys or other secrets to the repository.
